Life Hub

Life Hub is a private, self-hosted household app. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by the members of one household. It is not a public service: there is nothing to sign up for, and nobody outside that household can use it.

What it does with Google Drive

Life Hub keeps backup copies of its own database in its owner's Google Drive, so the household's records survive if the computer fails. Each copy is encrypted before it leaves the computer.

Life Hub asks Google for one permission only, drive.file. With it, Life Hub can see and manage only the backup files it created itself. It cannot see anything else in the Drive.
