Life Hub privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Life Hub is a private, self-hosted app used by one household. It runs on a computer the household owns. There is no Life Hub company, service or server that receives anyone's data.

Who can use it

Only the members of the household that runs it. Nobody else can sign in to it, and nobody else's Google account is ever connected to it.

What Life Hub accesses in a Google account

Life Hub connects to one Google account, its owner's, with the drive.file permission. That permission lets it create files in Google Drive and read, replace and delete only the files it created itself. It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive, and it accesses nothing else in the Google account.

How that access is used

Only to store and manage backups: Life Hub uploads encrypted backup copies of its own database, downloads one from time to time to test that it can be restored, and deletes old copies. It does nothing else with the access.

Sharing

Life Hub does not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. The backup files stay in the owner's own Google Drive. They are encrypted with a key that only the household holds.

Storage and deletion

The authorization Google issues is stored encrypted on the household's own computer. The owner can end the access at any time in their Google account's security settings (Third-party apps and services), and can delete the backup files from Google Drive at any time.

Google's Limited Use requirements

Life Hub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
